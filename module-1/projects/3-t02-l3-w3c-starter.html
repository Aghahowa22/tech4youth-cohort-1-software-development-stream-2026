What is Web Accessibility?

Web accessibility means making websites usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. This includes people who are blind, deaf, colorblind, or have motor disabilities.

Accessibility isn't just about people with disabilities. It benefits everyone: captions help in loud environments, large text helps in bright sunlight, and keyboard shortcuts help busy professionals.

WCAG 2.1 Guidelines

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) are the international standard for web accessibility. They're organized into four principles: POUR.

Perceptible - Information must be perceivable
Operable - Interface must be operable via keyboard
Understandable - Content must be understandable
Robust - Content must work with assistive technologies

WCAG has three levels: A (minimum), AA (standard), and AAA (enhanced).
